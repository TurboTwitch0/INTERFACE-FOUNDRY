# Hero artwork

`foundry-glass-stack.png` is original generated artwork created for Interface
Foundry: three floating mint and smoked-glass interface tiles. The image is stored
locally; the website does not depend on a remote image service.

The composition takes general inspiration from the mint product-page example in
the public cover of the [supplied Reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdshHpFpqdi/).
No Nike marks, shoe artwork, cartoon frames, or video footage are included.

The source cover was inspected; full video playback was unavailable. This is an
original interpretation of the cover's composition and color, not a reconstruction
of unseen video frames.
